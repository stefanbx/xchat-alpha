Run a relay — one command

A relay is what makes ӾChat hard to switch off: it keeps everyone's signed posts and hands them out. The more people run one, the less any single machine matters. You don't need to be technical — and you don't need a server. A spare laptop is fine.

  1. Open Terminal — on a Mac press ⌘ Space, type terminal, hit Enter.
  2. Tap the button, paste into the black window (⌘ V), press Enter.
  3. That's it. It prints your relay's web address, gives you a settings page and an xchat command, and starts itself every time you log in.
$curl -fsSL https://xchat-alpha-node.fly.dev/relay.sh | sh

It installs into one folder in your home directory and opens a free Cloudflare tunnel, so it works behind a home router — no ports to forward, no account to make. Nothing else on your machine is touched, and it holds no key to your money: a relay stores signed bytes, it can't post or spend as anyone. It does make itself a permanent identity on first run, so it stays the same relay to everyone else even when your machine's address changes. It stores a share of the network rather than a copy of everything, and if your computer has a GPU it also computes the proof-of-work that makes everyone's tips settle in seconds — which you get paid for, like pinning. Add --with-node to also run your own node, or --domain your.host for a fixed address. Read it before you run it — it's plain, commented shell, and that URL serves the same text in your browser. To remove it later: sh ~/.xchat-relay/install-relay.sh --uninstall.
